About this game

Beam Them Back is a goofy 1950s B-movie alien invasion, and you and up to three friends are the only thing standing between a flying saucer and the town it wants. Between waves you build: walls, turrets, generators, tar pits that trap a leg mid-stride, propane barrels that go off when you shoot them, and lures that pull a whole horde somewhere you chose. Fifteen waves a run, climbing in size and nerve.

Then you hold. The aliens are not chasing you, they are chasing the shelter, the townsfolk hiding behind whichever door you left thin. Empty the shelter and the run is over, even if every player is still standing.

Eight species come for different reasons and answer to different tricks. Five roadside places, five different defensive problems. Turrets run dry and need a hand on the trigger. Someone always needs rescuing. The run summary names who did what, in 1957's own voice.

In short

  • 15 waves, 5 roadside places, 8 alien species and a boss that lands every fifth wave.
  • Build by day, hold by night. Walls, turrets, tar pits, propane barrels and lures. The aliens are coming for the shelter, not for you.
  • Full 1–4 player co-op. Rejoin after a drop, spectate a downed teammate, ping what you see.
  • Nothing automatic wins alone. Turrets run dry and need a hand, and every species answers to a different trick.
  • Endless mode, three difficulties, full key rebinding, and a controller that can do everything a keyboard can.

Before you buy

Known limitations, honestly

We would rather tell you now than have you find out mid-wave.

  • No host migration. If the host quits, the run ends. Bring the whole squad back next time.
  • No minimap. There is a compass. Learn the doors, read the terrain.
  • English only, for now.
  • Windows only, for now. Steam Deck runs it through Proton.
