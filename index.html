How we make things

No ads. Ever.

You pay once, you get the game. Nothing inside it is trying to sell you anything.

No accounts, no tracking you did not say yes to.

Our games run without an account. Anonymous play-logs help us fix bugs, and they are off until you switch them on. The whole policy fits on one page.

Friends first.

Every game is built for the call with your friends: quick to host, quick to join, and it works with a controller from the title card to the credits.
